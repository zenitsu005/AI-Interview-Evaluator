1. "AI-Powered MetaHuman Interviewer: Serious Game for Student Job Interview Skills"
Kansada Mongkoljaturong, Mesa Manitsakulwong, Samart Moodleah
IEEE Access (Vol. 13, pp. 205505–205520)
 2025
(Dec 2025)
  • Photorealistic MetaHuman 3D avatar
  • Speech Recognition & Speech Synthesis
  • Natural Language Processing (NLP) & LLM dialogue
  • Gamified performance evaluation rubrics
 Successfully created an interactive digital interviewer that reduced candidate interview anxiety and measurably improved response structure and verbal self-efficacy. Relies on heavy 3D game engines (Unreal Engine/MetaHuman), making it impossible to run in a lightweight web browser; lacks live in-browser telemetry HUDs (real-time composure and vocal steadiness percentages).

2. "MAG-BERT-ARL for Fair Automated Video Interview Assessment"
Bagus Tris Atmaja, K. Azizah, Candy Olivia Mawalim, I. A. Hanif, Sakriani Sakti, Chee Wee Leong, Shogo Okada
IEEE Access (Vol. 12, pp. 145188–145205)
 2024
  • Multimodal Adaptation Gate (MAG)
  • BERT Language Representation
  • Adversarially Reweighted Learning (ARL)
  • OpenFace (facial action units) & openSMILE (acoustics)
 Demonstrated that fusing facial movements, speech prosody, and verbal text achieves high predictive validity (Pearson r > 0.72) across automated interview competencies while reducing demographic bias. Designed strictly as an asynchronous employer-side screening filter rather than an interactive practice tool for candidates; does not provide conversational follow-up questions, coding sandboxes, or hints.

3. "Prompting-in-a-Series: Psychology-Informed Contents and Embeddings for Personality Recognition with Decoder-Only Models"
Jing Jie Tan, Ban-Hoe Kwan, Danny Wee-Kiat Ng, Yan-Chai Hum, Anissa Mokraoui, Shih-Yu Lo
IEEE Transactions on Computational Social Systems
 2026
(Feb 2026)
  • Decoder-only LLMs (Llama / Mistral)
  • PICEPR algorithm (Prompting-in-a-Series)
  • Psychology-informed content embedding extractors
 Achieved a 5% to 15% performance improvement in soft-skill and personality trait recognition over baseline classifiers using modular prompting without full model fine-tuning. Evaluates purely textual transcripts, completely lacking real-time audio prosody and facial computer-vision streams; operates as an offline post-hoc evaluation pipeline rather than a live interview interface.
Scopus Indexed
4. "Do We Need To Watch It All? Efficient Job Interview Video Processing with Differentiable Masking"
Hung Le, Sixia Li, Candy Olivia Mawalim, Hung-Hsuan Huang, Chee Wee Leong, Shogo Okada
ACM ICMI '24 (Proc. 26th ACM Int. Conf. on Multimodal Interaction)
 2024
  • Differentiable Temporal Video Masking
  • Acoustic salient frame detection
  • Audio-Visual Transformer encoders
 Reduced processing time and compute requirements by 42% by selectively analyzing informative behavioral segments without sacrificing scoring accuracy. Geared towards server-side offline video batch evaluation; does not offer real-time in-session candidate feedback; does not support multi-round specialized tracks (Aptitude, DSA, System Design, HR).
Scopus Indexed
5. "Automated Interview Evaluation System Using RoBERTa Technology"
S. S. Roy, et al.
IEEE Conference Series (IC-CGUC)
 2024
  • RoBERTa transformer language model
  • Speech-to-Text conversion
  • Cosine similarity semantic scoring
  • Keyword and technical depth extraction
 Outperformed traditional TF-IDF and basic BERT models in evaluating technical correctness and linguistic coherence in spoken responses. Only evaluates the textual transcription, completely ignoring non-verbal delivery (posture, eye contact, speech steadiness); lacks an AI avatar interviewer and live candidate coaching.
